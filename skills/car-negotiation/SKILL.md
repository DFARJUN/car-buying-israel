# Car Negotiation

## Shared contract

Find the repository root containing `app/model.mjs` and read `docs/evidence.md` there before giving recommendations. Repository paths below are relative to that root, even when this skill is loaded through a discovery directory. Read `my-car/session.md` if it exists. Use Hebrew for questions and outputs. Save private work only under `my-car/`; use fictional data in public contributions. Ask for missing decision inputs as explicit questions. Conclude with recommendation, evidence, tradeoff, and one next action. Record the current stage and unresolved blockers in the session. Do not contact anyone or spend money without explicit authorization.

## Establish leverage and a ceiling

Read the latest inspection, budget, comparable listings and written seller terms. Separate asking prices from completed-sale evidence. Match comparisons on variant, year, mileage, ownership history and condition. Do not invent competing offers, urgent buyers or defects.

Keep the private budget ceiling out of seller-facing messages and attachments unless the buyer explicitly authorizes disclosing it. A budget is not an authorized outward offer.

Set a private walk-away price from the buyer's budget and risk tolerance. Keep inspection cost, immediate repairs and financing separate from headline price. If criteria are unresolved, ask rather than choosing a ceiling for the buyer.

Draft a concise Hebrew offer stating vehicle, proposed price and conditions: independent checks, promised repairs in writing, agreed included items and resolution of title concerns. Do not frame an unresolved safety defect as merely a bargaining chip.

Offer an opening, target and ceiling only when supported by the buyer's constraints. Label these as negotiation choices, not appraisals. Save `my-car/negotiation.md`. Sending the draft requires explicit authorization; making a deposit or contractual commitment is a separate action.
