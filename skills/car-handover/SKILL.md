# Car Handover

## Shared contract

Find the repository root containing `app/model.mjs` and read `docs/evidence.md` there before giving recommendations. Repository paths below are relative to that root, even when this skill is loaded through a discovery directory. Read `my-car/session.md` if it exists. Use Hebrew for questions and outputs. Save private work only under `my-car/`; use fictional data in public contributions. Ask for missing decision inputs as explicit questions. Conclude with recommendation, evidence, tradeoff, and one next action. Record the current stage and unresolved blockers in the session. Do not contact anyone or spend money without explicit authorization.

## Prepare a closing checklist

Read `docs/sources.md` and verify the current official transfer route, eligibility, documents and fee for this seller/buyer/vehicle. Do not hardcode a universal online process, payment order or legal rule. Inspect the latest identity/title checks, inspection resolution, written contract and insurance confirmation.

Agree with the parties and, where needed, a qualified professional on how payment, confirmed title transfer and release of the vehicle are coordinated. A screenshot is not proof of cleared payment. A verbal promise to clear a lien later does not resolve it. Do not share passwords, OTPs or banking access with the agent.

Use `templates/handover.md`. At delivery verify the exact vehicle/VIN, actual mileage, condition versus agreement, repairs, all keys, accessories/charging cables, documents, warranty start and service schedule. For new/zero-km, record delivery defects and registration details before acceptance.

Record amounts and confirmations privately only after the buyer reports completion. If title, insurance, identity or an agreed condition remains unresolved, state the blocker and next verification rather than declaring ready to buy. The checklist supports the buyer; it is not a legal contract or mechanical certification.
