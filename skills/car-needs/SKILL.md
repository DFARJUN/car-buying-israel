# Car Needs

## Shared contract

Find the repository root containing `app/model.mjs` and read `docs/evidence.md` there before giving recommendations. Repository paths below are relative to that root, even when this skill is loaded through a discovery directory. Read `my-car/session.md` if it exists. Use Hebrew for questions and outputs. Save private work only under `my-car/`; use fictional data in public contributions. Ask for missing decision inputs as explicit questions. Conclude with recommendation, evidence, tradeoff, and one next action. Record the current stage and unresolved blockers in the session. Do not contact anyone or spend money without explicit authorization.

## Build a usable brief

Ask about total cash available including immediate expenses, sustainable monthly spending, purchase deadline, ownership horizon, annual distance, urban/highway mix, passengers, child-seat or accessibility fit, parking dimensions, and charging access when relevant. Allow “unknown” and return to it when it affects a decision.

Distinguish hard constraints from preferences and testable fit requirements. Do not impose brand origin, transmission type, fuel type, family status or a previous buyer's budget. Ask which tradeoff the buyer would accept when constraints conflict; show evidence before claiming the market cannot satisfy a brief.

Agree on ranking weights for reliability, resale, space, safety and running cost. Use equal weights only as a clearly labelled starting point. Physical fit (seat, boot, parking) requires measurements or a visit, not a brochure volume alone.

Write `my-car/brief.md` with the confirmed answers, hard gates, preferences, unknowns, and what would change the brief. Copy those gates into the comparison settings without treating missing candidate data as a pass.
