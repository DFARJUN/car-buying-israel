# Car Offer Review

## Shared contract

Find the repository root containing `app/model.mjs` and read `docs/evidence.md` there before giving recommendations. Repository paths below are relative to that root, even when this skill is loaded through a discovery directory. Read `my-car/session.md` if it exists. Use Hebrew for questions and outputs. Save private work only under `my-car/`; use fictional data in public contributions. Ask for missing decision inputs as explicit questions. Conclude with recommendation, evidence, tradeoff, and one next action. Record the current stage and unresolved blockers in the session. Do not contact anyone or spend money without explicit authorization.

## Turn the offer into comparable terms

Read `templates/offer.md`. Confirm the exact variant, production/model/registration dates, advertised versus recorded ownership, previous use, stock status, delivery date, full payable price and which fees/accessories are mandatory. Do not assume “zero-km” means unregistered, first owner, or a full warranty starting today.

Extract warranty provider, start/end dates, distance limits, covered parts, exclusions, deductible, claim ceiling, maintenance obligations and transferability. Compare exclusions with the actual model's known risks; a long warranty headline alone does not remove those risks.

Capture deposit, refund/cancellation conditions, price-change clauses, delays, substitutions and promised repairs in writing. Do not infer statutory rights or cancellation periods; check current primary legal sources for the exact transaction or identify a need for qualified review.

Separate cash price, finance total and trade-in allowance. A high trade-in allowance can conceal a higher purchase price. Route loan terms to car-finance. Produce a missing-terms list and a request-for-clarification draft. Do not sign or pay on the buyer's behalf.
